# Lekha: a one-page guide for pilot users

Lekha answers questions from your company's approved documents and meetings, and shows you
exactly where each answer came from. It only ever uses material **you** are allowed to see.

## Ask
- Type a question in English or Kannada, in your own words: "How many days of leave do I
  get?", "What did we decide about forklift training?"
- Lekha shows its progress: searching, reading, writing, checking. Answers take a little
  while in this pilot.
- **Every answer has numbered sources.** Click one to read the exact passage, with the
  quoted words highlighted. For meetings you also see when it was said (e.g. "at 12:40").
- If the documents don't say, Lekha tells you **"I do not have enough information"** rather
  than guess. You can then **send the question to your curators**, who can add the missing
  policy. You'll see what they did under **Requests**.
- Use **Yes / No** under an answer to tell us whether it helped.

## Your meetings
- **Meetings** lists the meetings you took part in. Nobody else can find them, not even
  administrators who weren't there.
- To add one, import the transcript Teams gives you (.vtt), or an .srt or .txt file, and
  tick the colleagues who were there.
- **Draft minutes** asks Lekha to write a summary, decisions and action items. Every point
  quotes the transcript. Only the meeting's organiser can **approve** them; approved minutes
  become searchable for the participants.

## Glossary
- **Glossary** lists names that mean the same thing ("PL" = "privilege leave" = "annual
  leave"; "casual leave" = "ಸಾಂದರ್ಭಿಕ ರಜೆ"). Lekha uses them automatically, and shows "Also
  searched for…" when it does.

## For curators
- **Documents:** upload PDF or Word files and choose who may read them. Uploading a file with
  the same title creates a new version; readers see the new one once it's processed.
- **Glossary:** add terms and their other names, in any language. Share a term with the
  whole organisation, or keep it to your unit.
- **Requests:** questions people couldn't get answered. Fix the gap, then mark it resolved.

## Good to know
- Lekha never sends messages, creates tickets or takes any action on your behalf.
- Everything you ask is recorded in an audit log that can't be edited. Only auditors can
  read it, and it never shows your question text.
- **This pilot uses invented (synthetic) documents only.**
